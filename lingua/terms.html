1. What the app is

Lingua is a tool for inventing a language — its sounds, its dictionary, its alphabet, its writing system — and a timeline where those languages can be shared. The making side works on your own device. The timeline is online.

2. Your account

  • You are asked who you are — with Apple, Google or an email address — in two places only: before you post, and before you buy. A handle and a display name are required at that point, because a timeline of unnamed accounts is not a timeline.
  • Attaching an identity keeps the same account. Nothing you have made is copied, moved or lost at that moment.
  • One account per person. Making further accounts in order to get around a freeze is a breach of these terms.
  • You are responsible for what is done through your account.

4. Reporting, blocking, and what we do about it

  • Every post carries a report control, and every account page carries report and block. A blocked account's posts do not reach you.
  • We review every report within 24 hours.
  • A post that breaks these terms is taken down. It stops being visible to anybody else; where it was part of a conversation, the conversation says that it was removed.
  • An account that breaks these terms is frozen. A frozen account cannot post, reply, react, follow or report. It can still read, it can still build a language, and it can still be deleted by the person who owns it.
  • Repeated or severe breaches end in the account being deleted.
  • We may take any of these steps without prior notice.

If you believe a decision about your account is wrong, write to Lingua@tokinets.com. The app links to the same address from the screen that tells you the account is frozen.

5. What you make stays yours

  • By posting to the timeline you allow us to store and display that post to other people using the app, for as long as it is posted. That permission ends when you delete the post.
  • Publishing a language means giving other people a copy of it. We record when it was published; we do not police what anyone does with a copy they already have.

6. Where your work is kept

A language belongs to your account and is kept on our servers as well as on your device, so that a lost or replaced phone does not lose it. Nothing about this depends on payment. The app is expected to work with no signal: what is on the device is what you are working on, and the two copies are put together when the network returns.

7. Deleting things

  • An account can be deleted from the app, in Settings. Deleting an account removes it and everything of yours from our servers, and cannot be undone. The languages on your device are not touched by it.
