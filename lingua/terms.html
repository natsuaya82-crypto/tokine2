Lingua Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-08-28

Lingua is an iOS app for building your own language — its letters, its words, its grammar — and writing to other people in it. The app is made by Tokine, in Tokyo. These terms apply to everyone who uses it.

Lingua does not tolerate abusive posts or abusive behaviour. Where we find a post that hurts other people, or behaviour such as harassment or impersonation, we remove the post and suspend or delete the account, without notice.

1. Your account

  • You can create an account with an email address and a password, or by signing in with Apple or Google.
  • Your handle and display name are shown to other people using the app. Do not put anything in them you would not want other people to read.
  • Keep your sign-in details to yourself. You are responsible for what is done through your account.

2. What you make

The languages you build and the posts you write remain yours. You choose the licence for each language you make.

You are responsible for what you post, and you are responsible for having the right to post it.

3. What is not allowed

While using Lingua, you must not:

  • post anything that hurts other people — abuse, threats, discrimination, or sexual harassment;
  • post violent or obscene material, or material that encourages crime;
  • impersonate another person, or post another person's private information without their permission;
  • post advertising, solicitation, or spam;
  • break the law;
  • interfere with the running of the app, or tamper with its data by improper means.

4. Reporting and blocking

If you find an abusive post or an abusive person, report them from inside the app, or write to us at the address at the bottom of this page. You can also block a person, which is done from inside the app.

5. Removing posts and accounts

We may remove a post, and suspend or delete an account, without notice, where the rules above have been broken. Reports are read and acted on.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app, and deleting it deletes everything — your profile, your languages, your posts and drafts, and the rest of your records. This cannot be undone.

Lingua keeps backup files of your languages on your device, in the app's Documents folder, and they are included in your iOS backup. Keep your own copy of anything you would not want to lose.

7. Paid plans

Lingua's paid plans are Apple in-app purchases. Apple handles the payment, and refunds are made under Apple's rules, not ours.

8. No advertising

Lingua shows no ads, and contains no third-party analytics.

9. Changes to the app and to these terms

We may change what the app does, or stop offering part of it. We may also change these terms; the new version is published on this page, with a new date at the top, and applies from the moment it is published.

10. Disclaimer

  • We do not warrant that the app is free of faults, or that it is fit for any particular purpose.
  • Where data is lost through a fault in the app, a broken device, or a network failure, we cannot undertake to recover it.
  • Disputes between people using the app are between them.
