Lingua

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

1. Who this covers

This policy explains what Lingua does with information. Lingua is an app for building a constructed language — its words, its alphabet, its writing system — and for sharing what you write in it. It is made by Tokine (刻音).

This policy is about Lingua only. Tokine’s other apps are covered by their own policies.

2. What stays on your phone

The language you build is kept on your phone as you work: the words, the drawn letters, the writing system, the keyboard you lay out, your notes, and your settings. This copy is what lets the making side of the app work with no signal.

Lingua also writes a backup of the open language as a file in the app’s Documents folder, and it writes any voice you record for a post there as a file. Voice recordings are not uploaded unless you post them. Files in Documents are included in your device backup and are visible to you in the Files app.

Deleting the app deletes what is on the phone, including those files.

3. What you need an account for

Lingua needs an account. You can create one with an email address and a password, with Sign in with Apple, or with Sign in with Google.

We receive your email address. We do not receive or store your password: authentication is handled by Supabase, and a sign-in returns a token rather than a password. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email address, we receive only the relay address Apple gives us.

We do not ask for your name, your address, or your telephone number.

4. What is stored on the server

With an account, the following is stored on our server:

  • Your profile — the handle you choose, the display name you choose, the small picture you choose for yourself, and the date the account was made.
  • Your languages — the words, letters, writing system, keyboard, notes and other parts of each language you build, so that they survive losing the phone and so that you can carry on where you left off.
  • What you post — the text of a post, any photographs and any voice attached to it, which language it is written in, what it replies to, and when it was written.
  • What you do on the timeline — who you follow, who you block, what you react to, what you quote.
  • Reports — if you report a post or an account, the report is stored so it can be answered.

5. What other people can see

Handles, display names, profile pictures and posts are readable by other people using Lingua. That is what the timeline is.

Photographs and voice recordings attached to a post are stored in a publicly readable bucket. Anybody who has the file’s address can open it, whether or not they use Lingua. Please do not put anything in a post that you would not put on a public web page.

A language you build is private until you publish its page. You choose whether it has a page anybody can open, and whether its letters and words can be downloaded.

6. What we do not do

  • Lingua shows no advertising.
  • Lingua contains no analytics or tracking SDK. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and the app does not ask for tracking permission.
  • We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not build profiles of you for marketing.

7. Subscriptions

Lingua offers paid plans as auto-renewable subscriptions through the App Store. Apple is the seller. Payment is taken by Apple, and we never see your card number, your billing address, or any other payment detail. What reaches the app is whether a subscription is currently active.

A plan decides what you may do. It decides nothing about what exists: nothing you have made is removed, hidden or held back because a plan ended.

8. Who else handles this information

  • Supabase — the database, the file storage and the sign-in service behind the app. Everything in section 4 is stored there.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, and all subscription payments.
  • Google — Sign in with Google, if you use it.

Each handles information under its own terms and privacy policy.

9. How long it is kept

What is on the server is kept while your account exists. A post you delete is removed. A post taken down after a report is kept rather than deleted, so that the report can be answered and so that a wrong decision can be undone; it is no longer shown to other people.

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Deleting the account removes your profile, your languages, your posts, your reactions, your follows, and the photographs and voice recordings you uploaded. This cannot be undone.

The files are removed first and the account after. If the network fails part-way through, the account is still deleted — we will not leave an account alive because a request failed — and a file may be left behind. If that happens, write to us and we will remove it.

Deleting your account does not delete the copy on your phone, and deleting the app does not delete your account. They are two separate things and you can do either, or both.

11. Children

Lingua is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will remove it.

12. Where the information is held

Our server is operated by Supabase and the information described above may be stored and processed outside the country you are in.

13. Your rights

You can see and change your profile and your languages in the app, and you can delete everything by deleting your account. For anything else — a copy of what we hold, a correction we cannot make from the app — write to us at the address below.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change matters, we will say so in the app or on this page, and the date at the top will change.
