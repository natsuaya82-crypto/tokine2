Lingua Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-28

Lingua is an iOS app for building your own language — its letters, its words, its grammar — and writing to other people in it. The app is made by Tokine, in Tokyo. This page describes what Lingua stores, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

Lingua shows no ads, and contains no third-party analytics. We do not sell your information.

1. Your account

You can create an account with an email address and a password, or by signing in with Apple or Google.

The app does not keep your password. It is sent to Supabase, our server provider, and what remains on your device is a sign-in token.

2. What is stored on the server

Lingua's server and database are provided by Supabase. These are the records Lingua keeps there:

ProfileYour account: identifier, handle (@name), display name, profile picture, and the date it was created. It also carries a mark saying whether the account answers reports, which we set and you cannot.
LanguagesEach language you make: its name, the licence you choose for it, and its dates.
Language contentsWhat a language is made of: words, letters, writing systems, and the rest.
PostsWhat you post: the text, photos and audio in it, and the prompt it answers, if any.
DraftsPosts you have not sent. Only you can read your drafts.
Saved searchesSearch terms you have saved.
ReactionsLikes and reposts.
FollowsWho you follow.
BlocksWho you have blocked.
ReportsReports you send about a post or a person.
PromptsThe daily prompt everyone is invited to write to.

Two further records, quotes and publications, exist in the database but are not in use yet.

3. What is stored on your device

Lingua keeps its own copy of nearly everything on the phone, so that it works with no signal. The server is where these things live; the phone is where they are made and read.

  • Your languages in full — the words, the letters, the writing system, the sounds, the keyboard, the notes, and the rest of what a language is made of.
  • Your profile, including three parts of it that stay on the phone and never reach the server: the line you write about yourself, your link, and your location.
  • Your posts and your drafts.
  • Your sign-in token, which is what keeps you signed in. Your password is not kept.
  • Your app settings.
  • In the app's Documents folder: backup files of your languages, the recordings you make, and the sheets Lingua writes. Because they are kept there, they are included in your iOS backup.

4. What other people can see

Lingua is a place where people write to each other, so the things you publish — your handle and display name, your posts, the languages you share, and who you follow — are visible to other people using the app. Your drafts are not.

The photos and audio in a post are stored openly. Anyone holding the address of one can read it — including someone who is not using Lingua, and from outside the app. The text of a post is not stored this way; the photos and audio are.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app, and deleting it deletes everything. Your profile, your languages, your posts and drafts, your saved searches, reactions, follows and blocks are removed. This cannot be undone.

One thing is kept: the reports you have sent about other people remain, with your name taken off them. A report is a record about somebody else, and deleting your account would otherwise withdraw every report you had ever made.

The files Lingua has written on your device — the backups of your languages, the recordings, and the sheets it has made — are removed as well, in the same act.

6. Purchases

Lingua's paid plans are Apple in-app purchases. Apple handles the payment. We never receive or store your card or payment details.

7. Children

Lingua is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly keep information about anyone under 13, and if we find that we do, we delete it together with the account. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has an account, write to us at the address below.
