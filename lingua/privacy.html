Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

This policy describes what Lingua (the app), provided by TOKINETS (we, us), does with information.

We do not ask for your name, your address, your phone number or your date of birth, and we do not collect them. We run no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and we sell nothing to anybody.

1. The account

The first time you open the app, an anonymous account is created for you. It is an identifier and a pair of tokens, and nothing else — no address, no password, nothing that says who you are. It exists so that the language you make belongs to something.

You are asked to attach an identity in two places only: before you post to the timeline, and before you buy anything. Attaching one keeps the same account.

What we then hold depends on how you sign in:

Apple An identifier from Apple, and an email address — which is a private relay address if you chose to hide yours. We never see your Apple password.
Google An identifier from Google, and your email address. We never see your Google password.
Email The address, and a one-way hash of your password held by our authentication provider. The password itself never reaches us and is never stored by the app.

The email address is used to confirm the account and to let you recover it. We do not send marketing.

2. What is on your device only

Unless you sign in, everything below stays on the phone and reaches no server:

  • your languages — words, meanings, sounds, letters, drawn shapes, writing systems, keyboards, grammar, notes;
  • the app's settings, and which plan the device believes it has;
  • a backup file of each language, written into the Files app on your own device.

3. What is on our servers

  • Your account: the identifier above, your handle, your display name, the small drawn mark shown beside your posts, and your profile line if you write one.
  • Your languages: a language belongs to your account and is kept on our servers as well as on your device, so that a lost or replaced phone does not lose it. Only you can read it. A language you have not published is readable by nobody else, and neither is what it is made of.
  • What you post: the text, the letters as shapes, any photographs and any voice recording. These are public — anybody using the app can see them.
  • What you do on the timeline: who you follow, what you liked or boosted, who you blocked, and any report you send.

Photographs and voice recordings on a post are stored as files. A photograph is resized before it leaves the phone, and any letters you placed on it are drawn into the image itself. Location data attached by your camera does not travel: the app re-encodes the image, which drops it.

4. Who else sees it

Nobody, except the people using the app who can see what you posted. We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties, and we run no advertising network. We disclose information only where the law requires it.

5. Services we use

Supabase Accounts, the database and file storage. Everything in section 3 is held there. Privacy policy
Apple Sign in with Apple, and all purchases. We never receive your payment details. Privacy policy
Google Sign in with Google, if you choose it. Privacy policy

6. How long it is kept

What is on our servers is kept while the account exists. Delete the account and it goes. Reports are kept after the account that made them is deleted, without the reporter's identity, because a report is about somebody else and deleting your own account should not withdraw a record about a third party.

7. Deleting your data

  • A post: delete it in the app. Its photographs and voice recording go with it.
  • Your account: Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes the account and everything of yours from our servers, and cannot be undone. It does not touch the languages on your device — erasing those is a separate button, and it says which is which.
  • If you cannot reach the app, write to Lingua@tokinets.com and we will delete it for you.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. If we learn that we hold information from a child under 13, we delete it. Where you are, a higher age may apply, and that age applies.

9. Your rights

You may ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to the address below.

10. Changes

We may change this policy. The changed policy takes effect when it is published on this page, and the date at the top is the date it changed.
